Privacy policy.

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Information we may collect

If you contact us or submit an enquiry/order form, we may collect your name, phone number, email address (if provided), delivery address and the details of your crop/product enquiry.

How we use it

We use submitted information only to respond to your enquiry, discuss crop/seed availability, communicate about a requested order, and provide customer support. We do not claim to sell personal data.

Storage and sharing

Information submitted through forms may be sent to our service/backend provider and stored there so we can respond. The demo site currently has no live backend connected, so form/order functionality may not be operational. We do not publish submitted personal information. We share it only with service providers needed to operate the service or when legally required.

Retention and your choices

We aim to retain enquiry information only as long as needed for the purpose or applicable legal requirements. You may request access, correction, deletion or withdraw consent by contacting us through the contact details made available by Seedshop. A contact channel is not yet configured on this demo.

India privacy context

This policy is intended to reflect basic transparency, purpose limitation, data minimisation and consent principles associated with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). This is a starter policy and not legal advice or a declaration of full compliance. Applicable provisions and rules may be subject to commencement and implementation.

Changes

We may update this policy when the site or data handling changes. The current version will be posted here.
